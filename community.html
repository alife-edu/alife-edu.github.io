ALife-Edu Community

A permanent home for ALife educators

The ALife-Edu community is a persistent space for educators, researchers, and practitioners who teach Artificial Life — independent of any single institution, conference, or platform.

01

The Discord server

Where the community gathers

The AEDU workshop is a starting point, not an endpoint. One of its core outcomes is launching a Discord server that gives this community a persistent place to gather — independent of any single institution, conference, or platform.

The server is designed around the work of teaching: sharing syllabi and assignments, workshopping course ideas, coordinating around conferences like ALIFE, and finding collaborators for education research. It's also a space for the broader community of people interested in ALife pedagogy, not just those who could attend the workshop.

The server launched on May 18, 2026 — Day 1 of the workshop — and is now open to everyone. Regular community meet-ups are organized through the server; upcoming dates are listed in the Events tab on the Discord.

02

Monthly meetups

The community gathers monthly for open video meetups — two separate sessions scheduled to be accessible across time zones. Upcoming dates and connection details are posted in the Events tab on the Discord server.

West session
Americas & Europe Meetup
Friendly for UTC−8 to UTC+2  ·  Monthly
An open video call for community members in North and South America and Western Europe. Discuss ongoing projects, share teaching ideas, and connect with fellow ALife educators.
See upcoming dates on Discord ↗
East session
Asia & Pacific Meetup
Friendly for UTC+5 to UTC+12  ·  Monthly
An open video call for community members in Asia, Australia, and the Pacific. Same format, timezone-shifted — so everyone gets a session at a reasonable hour. Bring questions, ideas, or just come to listen.
See upcoming dates on Discord ↗
03

Get involved

◇   Attend

Show up to a meetup or workshop

The monthly community meetups (East and West sessions) are the easiest way in — no registration needed, just join via the Discord Events tab. The TAL in-person workshop at ALIFE 2026 in Waterloo (August 2026) is the next major community gathering.

◇   Contribute

Share resources

Have a syllabus, assignment, simulation, or course you'd like to share? The community is building a shared collection of open pedagogical resources for ALife education.

◇   Collaborate

Co-organize

Interested in helping shape future workshops, contributing to the resource collection, or co-authoring education research? Connect with us through the Discord server.
