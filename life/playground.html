Life playground

These are the three Game of Life scenes from the ALife-Edu home page, on their own. Pause, slow them down to ¼ speed, or press Step to go one generation at a time. Open "Starting pattern as RLE" to copy any scene into Golly or LifeViewer and check it yourself.

The only rule

BirthA dead cell with exactly 3 live neighbours comes alive. SurvivalA live cell with 2 or 3 live neighbours stays alive. OtherwiseThe cell is dead in the next generation.

Each cell has eight neighbours, diagonals included, and every cell updates at the same moment. After generation 0 nothing is scripted. The page only applies this rule, then compares its final state with the one stored in the scene file. A ✓ in the readout means they match.

The scenes

  • Turner chain. A single glider meets a series of one-time turners, small still lifes that send it off in a new direction and are destroyed doing it. Four of the collisions leave a 2×2 block behind; on the home page those blocks are links. Here they're labelled Block 1 to 4.
  • ALIFE·EDU. 142 gliders start outside the window. They arrive in pairs, and each pair collides into one block (a glider synthesis), writing the words one pixel at a time.
  • Soup. A 20×20 square of random cells, about half of them alive, run until they settle into still lifes and oscillators. Click or drag on the grid to draw, press Clear to start from nothing, or try another seed.

How the scenes were found

The starting patterns came from small search programs in life/tools/. They test reactions, chain them together, and simulate every step to make sure it works. The README explains each method and lists the references behind them.
