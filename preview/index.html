Our Mission

Because of ALife's distribution across fields, many people teaching ALife classes don't have the chance to talk with any other ALife educators. That means that they have to reinvent teaching ALife from the ground up and we end up duplicating work and lacking a shared understanding of core learning goals.

To remedy that problem, we ran a free virtual workshop in May 2026, held in two time zones. Sessions ranged from “Reaching (for) Critical Maths” to “Grading as it could be”, and participants drafted learning goals together in a brainwriting activity. We met again at ALIFE 2026 in Waterloo and have continued to develop shared learning goals and materials ever since.

Anyone who teaches, or wants to teach, the principles of living systems is enthusiastically welcome. Come to an event, or find us on Discord and join the mailing list through the community page.

About this site

This site is run by the ALife-Edu community. We are purposefully independent of any one institution, society, conference, or lab so that we can continue growing this space far into the future.

The Spring 2026 workshop was organized by educators from Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology, Carleton College, the University of Warwick, Cross Labs, and In Silence AI. If you'd like to contribute resources or help organize a future event, get in touch on Discord or the mailing list.
