There's no shared ALife curriculum yet

Most people who teach artificial life are doing it on their own. There are no common learning goals and few places to ask what has worked in someone else's classroom, whether that's an intro CS course, a simulation lab, high-school outreach, or an industry training program.

ALife-Edu started with a free virtual workshop in May 2026, held in two time zones. Sessions ranged from “Reaching (for) Critical Maths” to “Grading as it could be”, and participants drafted learning goals together in a brainwriting activity. We met again at ALIFE 2026 in Waterloo and now host monthly talks.

Anyone who teaches, or wants to teach, the principles of living systems is welcome. Come to an event, or find us on Discord and the mailing list through the community page.

About this site

This site is run by the ALife-Edu community, independent of any one institution, conference, or lab.

The Spring 2026 workshop was organized by educators from Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology, Carleton College, the University of Warwick, Cross Labs, and In Silence AI. If you'd like to contribute resources or help organize a future event, get in touch on Discord or the mailing list.
